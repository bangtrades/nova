# Sparky Rename — Acronym Name Exploration

## Design Criteria

The name needs to:
- Be a real word or name that sounds natural when spoken by a 4-year-old AND a 14-year-old
- Unpack into a meaningful acronym describing what the platform does
- Feel techy and cool, not childish or cutesy
- Work as both the AI buddy character name AND the app brand
- Be short enough to say quickly ("Hey ___!")
- Have no major trademark conflicts in ed-tech, kids apps, or AI
- Look good as an app icon and wordmark

---

## Tier 1 — Strongest Candidates

### N.O.V.A. — Neural Observation & Visual Assistant

| Dimension | Score |
|-----------|-------|
| Kid-friendliness | "Hey Nova!" — natural, easy to say, sounds like a friend |
| Ages well | Cosmic, powerful, never embarrassing. Nova means "new" in Latin |
| Techy cred | Neural + Visual nails the AI learning product |
| Brand potential | Nova Kids / Nova Companion / "What's your Nova learning today?" |
| Trademark risk | PBS has "Nova" (science show) — different category but notable. Nova is widely used as a generic word though |
| App Store search | Common word, may need "Nova AI" or "Nova Learn" for discoverability |
| Character vibe | A glowing, star-shaped AI companion. Animations of light/energy. Color palette: deep space blues + bright stellar gold |

**Tagline:** *"Every lesson is a new star."*

## My Top 5 Recommendation (considering all factors)

1. **N.O.V.A.** — Best overall balance. Cosmic brand world, instantly recognizable, ages beautifully, strong character potential
2. **V.O.L.T.** — Cleanest trademark landscape. Electric energy brand, extremely memorable, great character (lightning bolt buddy)
3. **N.E.O.** — Coolest factor. Matrix cultural cachet, "new" meaning, sharp geometric brand potential
4. **R.U.N.E.** — Most unique. Zero competition in the name space, mystical-tech fusion is distinctive, fantastic character potential
5. **A.D.A.** — Best story. Ada Lovelace heritage gives you a built-in origin story that gets more powerful as the kid learns history
