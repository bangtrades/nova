**Medium — 4 options. Application-level probe.**

- **Option count:** exactly 4.
- **Distractor gap:** the three wrong options each map to a different
  common misconception. No "near-miss" is a pure surface variant —
  every one of them represents a partial understanding the child might
  genuinely hold.
- **Cognitive load:** application. The question presents a small
  scenario the child has not seen before and asks which outcome or
  explanation best fits. This requires *using* the concept, not just
  recognizing it.
- **Distractor design:**
    1. **Surface-correct, deep-wrong** — looks right because it uses
       the vocabulary, but misapplies the underlying rule.
    2. **Partially-right** — the first half of the answer is correct,
       but the causal direction or scope is reversed.
    3. **Adjacent-concept swap** — describes a related but distinct
       concept the child might confuse with this one.
- **Calibration target:** a child who can apply the concept to a new
  situation should score 60–75% on this; success here is a meaningful
  mastery signal.
