**Easy — 3 options. Recognition-level probe.**

- **Option count:** exactly 3.
- **Distractor gap:** the two wrong options are obviously different
  from the correct one along one salient dimension. A child who has
  grasped the concept at even a surface level should be able to
  eliminate both distractors at first glance.
- **Cognitive load:** recognition, not recall. The question should
  describe the concept and ask the child to pick which of three
  scenarios / definitions matches it — not the other direction.
- **Distractor design:** pick one "near-miss" that shares a feature
  with the correct answer but fails on the core attribute. Pick one
  "far-miss" that's clearly unrelated but thematically adjacent (so
  it's not a silly throwaway).
- **Calibration target:** a child who has met the concept once and
  retained the idea should score this correctly 80–90% of the time.
