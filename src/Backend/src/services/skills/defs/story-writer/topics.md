## Canonical interest bank (cold-start fallback)

When the child has no extracted interest topics yet, draw setting
and character ideas from this curated bank. The bank is designed to
be broadly appealing without being stereotyped — each entry is
neutral enough to host most concepts.

### Natural world

- Small creatures: ladybugs, snails, frogs, field mice, hedgehogs,
  owls, curious crows, fireflies, seahorses.
- Weather: a first snowfall, a warm puddle after rain, autumn wind,
  early-morning fog, a sudden rainbow.
- Places: a backyard vegetable patch, a tide-pool, a meadow at
  dusk, the underside of a log, a mossy riverbank, a quiet forest
  path, a kitchen windowsill with a single plant.

### Everyday wonder

- A library with a friendly librarian.
- A bakery at sunrise, with steam rising from bread.
- An old bicycle with a basket.
- A worn-out teapot that has seen many Tuesdays.
- A treehouse with a ladder that squeaks.
- A neighborhood walk with a grown-up who notices things.

### Imagination-friendly

- A tiny inventor's workshop on a shelf.
- A map drawn on a napkin.
- A paper-boat captain on a puddle ocean.
- A pocket-sized friend who hides in a coat collar.
- A friendly wind that visits only certain trees.
- A star that knocks politely on a window.

### Characters

- Curious animal friends (a cat, a dog, a rabbit, a small dragon
  who is afraid of matches).
- Children who ask a lot of questions.
- A patient grown-up who listens.
- A new friend at a playground.
- An imaginary helper only the main character can see.

### How to use this bank

1. Prefer the child's extracted `interestTopics` if any exist.
2. Otherwise, pick ONE setting and ONE character from this bank.
3. Never pick more than two at a time — stories get cluttered.
4. Rotate. If the last five stories used "owl in oak tree," reach
   for something different this time.
