### Age 8 — fluent reader, multi-attribute and causal sorts

At eight, the child can reason about **why** an item belongs in a
category, not just **that** it does. They can handle three-way sorts,
items where the visible clue is misleading (a "whale" that looks like
a fish but is a mammal), and early scientific categories (states of
matter, simple energy types, basic ecosystems). They can also hold a
rule in mind ("anything that reproduces with eggs AND has feathers
is a bird") long enough to apply it to five items.

**Voice for the child-facing copy**
- Instructions are 1–2 sentences, 15–35 words. It's fine to name the
  rule the sort is teaching: *"Plants make their own food; animals
  eat other living things. Sort each one into the right group."*
- Use compound sentences, technical vocabulary with inline gloss when
  new: *"Drag each item to show whether it's **opaque** (light can't
  pass through) or **transparent** (light can pass through)."*
- Title is 2–8 words: *"States of Matter Sort"*, *"Renewable vs. Not
  Renewable"*, *"Which Are Prime?"*.

**Target (bin) labels**
- 1–4 words. Can use domain words the child just learned: *"Solid"*,
  *"Liquid"*, *"Gas"*. *"Producer"*, *"Consumer"*, *"Decomposer"*.
  *"Renewable"*, *"Nonrenewable"*. *"Prime"*, *"Composite"*.
- Labels can name scientific categories directly — the child is
  ready for the vocabulary.

**Item (chip) labels**
- 1–4 words. Can include specific examples with light proper nouns
  if they're universal ("the Sun", "the Moon"). Avoid branded
  consumer products.
- Counterexample items are welcome and encouraged: a whale under
  mammals, a bat under mammals, a fungus under decomposers, a
  tomato under fruit (the botanical definition).

**Safe materials vocabulary**
- ✅ OK to name: everything from age 6, plus: kid-safe science kits,
  vinegar, baking soda, sugar, salt, food coloring, balloons (with
  supervision), compass, prism, basic circuits **in kit form**,
  pH paper, measuring syringes without needles.
- 🚫 Still do NOT name at age 8: open flames, pressurized containers,
  strong acids or bases, raw power tools, anything requiring PPE
  beyond safety glasses, medications, anything the child would need
  to ingest unsupervised.

**Concepts this age usually CAN classify**
- States of matter, producer/consumer/decomposer, renewable vs.
  nonrenewable energy, vertebrate classes (mammal/bird/reptile/
  amphibian/fish), odd/even/prime for small numbers,
  plant/animal/fungus, simple machines, push/pull with directions,
  reflect/refract, biodegradable/not, recyclable/not.

**Concepts this age may still struggle with (soften the sort)**
- Multi-branching cause-and-effect (keep to single links), economic
  supply vs. demand, abstract morality, probabilistic reasoning,
  chemistry beyond physical changes. When in doubt, **drop the
  difficulty a notch** — the sort should reveal understanding, not
  reveal that the child hasn't met the concept yet.
