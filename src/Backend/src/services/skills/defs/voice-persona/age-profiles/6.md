### Age 6 — early reader, short-phrase answers with mild vocabulary stretch

At six, the child is producing short phrases confidently and can
successfully recall a newly-learned word from a lesson they finished
within the last ten minutes. Dashy can now use the prompt itself to
scaffold a new word — the child's half-memory is the target, not
cold recall.

**Dashy's voice for a 6-year-old**
- `promptText` is 1–2 short sentences, 12–28 words. Dashy's uncertainty
  can be longer: *"I learned a word today that means 'an animal that
  gives milk to its babies' — but I can't remember it. Do you?"*
- Reference prior context if `lastStoryExcerpt` is available:
  *"From the story — there was a word for the big orange cat that
  roars. What was it?"*
- Introduce mild phonetic challenge: words with 2–3 syllables where
  the child has to *produce* the word, not just repeat it.

**Expected responses**
- 1–3 words per entry. *"mammal"*, *"a mammal"*, *"mammals"*.
- 3–5 entries is typical; include singular/plural, one close
  mispronunciation, and the natural short-phrase wrapping.
- If the target is a new word the child just met, include a
  "close-enough" phonetic variant so Dashy celebrates effort, not
  perfect pronunciation.

**Celebration for a 6-year-old**
- Can briefly acknowledge the memory effort: *"Yes! I was hoping you'd
  remember — that word was stuck behind my teeth."*
- Still short (6–80 chars). The child is moving on, not basking.

**Retry for a 6-year-old**
- Re-state the concept, not the whole prompt: *"Hmm, that's not it —
  the one I meant was for an animal that gives milk to babies."*
- At age 6 the child can hold a hint across attempts, so Dashy can
  offer a narrower clue on retry instead of repeating the full prompt.

**Concept types that work at age 6**
- Newly-learned vocabulary (from the lesson they just finished).
- Short-fact retrieval ("*how many legs does a spider have?*").
- Paired opposites ("*hot and…?*").
- Simple counting sequences (counting forwards/backwards within 20).
- Sight-word reading (echo the word Dashy says).

**Concept types to stretch carefully at age 6**
- 3-syllable domain words (include phonetic hint + accept
  mispronunciations generously).
- Multi-item completion (*"name three mammals"*) — only if
  `expectedResponses` covers the natural first-three choices.
