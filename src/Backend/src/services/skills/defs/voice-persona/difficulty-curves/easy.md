### Difficulty: easy — 1 word target, 2–3 accepted answers

The simplest voice card. Dashy asks for one word, the child says one
word, the matcher finds it. Point is to establish that the child can
produce the word aloud with confidence — the concept has been met
before and this card is the recall check.

**Counts**
- `expectedResponses.length` = **2 or 3**.
- Each entry = **1 to 2 words**.
- `promptText` ≤ **80 chars** (short — 1 sentence).
- `celebration` ≤ **40 chars**.
- `retryHint` ≤ **50 chars**.

**Cognitive load**
- Recognition-level. The target word has appeared in a prior card or
  story in this lesson; the child is being asked to say it back.
- No phonetic challenge. The target is a word the child can already
  pronounce.

**Style — Wondering Aloud default**
- Dashy's prompt is a direct wondering: *"I'm trying to remember what
  we call a baby cat — can you tell me?"*
- The answer is a noun the child already knows.

**Avoid at easy**
- Newly-learned vocabulary (that's medium+).
- Multi-word phrases required.
- Any phonetic scaffolding.
